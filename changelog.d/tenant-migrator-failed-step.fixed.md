- `TenantMigrator.migrateAll()` lists a tenant whose migration step failed under `failed` (with `error`, `version`, `direction` and the full `output`) instead of `success`. The migrator reports a failed step in its output rather than throwing, so the tenant used to be counted as migrated. The migrator's output text is unchanged; `Migrator.$lastStepFailure()` exposes the failed step as data.
