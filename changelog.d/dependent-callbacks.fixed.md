- `delete(callbacks=false)` now also skips the callbacks of the dependent records it deletes or removes through `hasMany()` / `hasOne()` `dependent=`, including their `afterFind` / `afterInitialization` and transaction callbacks; a `hasOne` with `dependent="deleteAll"` or `"removeAll"` (which loads the record) is covered too. `deleteAll(instantiate=true)`, `deleteOne()` and `updateOne()` with `callbacks=false` no longer run `afterFind` / `afterInitialization` on the records they load, like `updateAll(instantiate=true)`. An invalid `dependent` value on `hasMany()` or `hasOne()` now throws `Wheels.InvalidArgument` when the association is declared, instead of at the first delete
