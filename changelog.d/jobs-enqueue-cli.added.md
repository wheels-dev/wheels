- `wheels jobs enqueue <JobName>` adds a job to the queue from the command line: now, after `--in=<seconds>`, or at `--at=<ISO 8601 time>`, with `--data` (a JSON object), `--queue` and `--priority`. It accepts only a component under `app/jobs/` (or a `jobClassPrefixes` path) that extends `wheels.Job`, checked before the class is loaded. Scheduling here is a one-off delayed run; there is no recurring schedule.
