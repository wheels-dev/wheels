- Under CommandBox, a request whose path contains a backslash (`\` or `%5C`) now gets a 400 instead of a 500 from the servlet container. The fix is a new "Reject a backslash in the path" rule in the shipped `public/urlrewrite.xml` (and the `wheels new` template). Existing CommandBox apps can copy that rule from a new app's `public/urlrewrite.xml` into their own, ahead of the "Wheels pretty URLs" rule. `wheels start` (LuCLI on Tomcat) already answers 400 (#4114).
