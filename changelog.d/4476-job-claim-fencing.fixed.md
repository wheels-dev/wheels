- A background job whose claim was reaped as stale can no longer overwrite the attempt that replaced it. Every claim now writes a fresh `wheels_jobs.claimToken` (and the claiming host to `claimedBy`, both added to existing tables automatically), and a worker's completion, retry or failure applies only while the row still carries its own token. Before, a stalled worker that finished after its job was requeued and claimed again matched the new attempt's `processing` row: it marked the job `completed` (or requeued it) while the second attempt was still running, and that attempt's own result then matched nothing. A fenced result is discarded and logged as `Wheels.Job.Fenced`. `JobWorker.processNext()` returns `fenced: true` and `processQueue()` reports a `fenced` count. Covers both `JobWorker` and `processQueue()`. Fencing holds once every worker sharing the table runs this version. ([#4476](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/4476))
