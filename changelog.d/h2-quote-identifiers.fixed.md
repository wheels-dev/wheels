- On H2, models now quote identifiers that can't be written unquoted, H2 keywords (`order`, `group`, `value`, ...) and names with characters other than letters, digits and underscores, so such columns can be queried and ordered by. Ordinary names stay unquoted and resolve exactly as before, including with `DATABASE_TO_UPPER=FALSE`. A quoted name is upper-cased to match H2's default case folding (#4440)
