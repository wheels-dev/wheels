- `pluralize()` and `singularize()` cache their results per application, so a repeated word no longer rebuilds the irregular and uncountable lists and the rule table, or runs the rule regexes. Measured per call: 86 µs → 5 µs on RustCFML, about 300 µs → 10 µs on Adobe ColdFusion 2023 / 2025, 152 µs → 12 µs on BoxLang and 25 µs → 5 µs on Lucee 7. The cache is cleared when `set()` changes `uncountables` or `irregulars`, notices a word added to or removed from those settings directly (change an existing irregular through `set()`), is skipped when the current tenant overrides them, keeps a case variant of a word separate, caches only words of up to 100 characters and holds at most 2,000 entries (#4150)
