- `wheels upgrade check` checks the 4.1 → 4.2 items when an app below 4.2.0 targets 4.2.0 or later, and points at the 4.1 → 4.2 guide instead of reporting "no new breaking changes". Items that fail at runtime on 4.2 (an environment selected without `WHEELS_ENV`, an invalid `joinType`, `condition`/`unless` shapes 4.2 rejects, a `renderNotFound` route, `tinyInt1isBit=false`) fail the check; the rest (event templates, `/vendor` in `.gitignore`, `returnAs="structs"`, `X-Forwarded-Proto` without `trustProxyHeaders`, and others) are advisory.
