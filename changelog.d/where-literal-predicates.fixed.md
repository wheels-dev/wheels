- `where=`: a comparison between two numbers, such as `1 = 0` or `1 = 1`, now goes into the SQL unbound instead of throwing `Wheels.ColumnNotFound` for a column named `1`. A literal compared with another table's column inside a subquery (`id IN (SELECT book_id FROM accesses WHERE user_id = 1)`) now throws `Wheels.UnbindableWhereValue`, naming the condition and the alternatives (`whereIn()` with a value array, or running the subquery first), instead of `Array index [1] out of range`. A `Wheels.ColumnNotFound` from a `where` with a subquery now says the same.
