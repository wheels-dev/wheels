- A polymorphic association without `foreignKey` / `foreignType` now finds its columns on the child's table like other associations do: `<name>id` / `<name>type` when they exist, otherwise `<name>_id` / `<name>_type`, which `t.references(polymorphic=true)` writes when `useUnderscoreReferenceColumns` is on (the `wheels new` default). Before, those apps threw `key [<name>id] doesn't exist`. When both shapes exist, the names without the underscore win
