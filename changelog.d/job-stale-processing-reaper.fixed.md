- A job left in `processing` by a crashed or killed worker is now recovered during normal polling. `JobWorker.processNext()` (the loop behind `wheels jobs work`) runs the stale-processing check before claiming, so a job stuck in `processing` is requeued for retry (counting the attempt) or marked failed when retries are exhausted, instead of staying stuck forever. The reap is scoped to the queues the poll serves and only acts once a job has been idle past a grace margin (`timeout + max(60s, timeout)`), so a worker never reaps another worker's live job, and the requeue is an atomic conditional `UPDATE` guarded by the attempt count a claim bumps — two workers polling the same queue cannot double-requeue or double-count the same job. Recovery is at-least-once: workers sharing a queue should configure the same `timeout`. ([#3888](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/3888))
