- A job left in `processing` by a crashed or killed worker is now recovered during normal polling. `JobWorker.processNext()` (the loop behind `wheels jobs work`) runs the stale-processing check before claiming, so a job stuck in `processing` beyond its `timeout` is requeued for retry (counting the attempt) or marked failed when retries are exhausted, instead of staying stuck forever. ([#3888](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/3888))
