- Nested properties now only update or delete child rows that belong to the record being saved. A nested key that names a row of another record is treated as not found (it isn't changed, moved or deleted), the child's foreign key always comes from the parent's saved key, and posted primary-key values aren't kept on a new child except the join row's own foreign keys. This applies to `hasMany`, `hasOne` and nested `belongsTo` associations, including custom `joinKey` associations; keys are compared exactly (string keys such as `01` and `1` are different, and BIGINT keys are compared without rounding), and nested struct keys above 2^31 on BIGINT columns are looked up instead of being treated as new rows
