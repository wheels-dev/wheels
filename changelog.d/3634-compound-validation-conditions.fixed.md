- Model validations: `condition`/`unless` expressions that combine clauses with `&&`/`||` no longer throw `Unsupported operator in condition`. `$evaluateConditionString()` now splits a compound boolean at the top level (quote- and paren-aware, with `&&` binding tighter than `||`) and evaluates each side with the existing single-clause machinery, and it recognises a small whitelist of read-only function calls with arguments, including the idiomatic `StructKeyExists(this, 'property')`. Previously any such expression fell through to the three-token `$evaluateLogicalExpression()` parser, which read the wrong token as the operator; because 4.1.0 made that path fail closed, the symptom changed from a silently skipped validation to a thrown `Wheels.InvalidValidationCondition` (HTTP 500 on save) (#3634)
