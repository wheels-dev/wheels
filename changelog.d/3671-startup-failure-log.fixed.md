- When Wheels fails to initialize, the minimal "Application Error" page no longer points at a log entry that does not exist: `onError()`'s fallback now writes the failure to `wheels.log` (type `error`) before rendering, walking `RootCause`/`Cause` so the real type, message, detail and first tag-context frame are logged even when Adobe CF wraps them in an event-handler exception. The page itself stays minimal and now names `wheels.log`. Existing apps must copy the change into their own `public/Application.cfc`: replace `$renderMinimalError()` and add `$logStartupFailure()`, `$startupFailureCause()`, `$startupFailureField()` and `$startupFailureFrame()` from the current `wheels new` template, and pass `arguments.EventName ?: ""` as the second argument at both `$renderMinimalError()` calls in `onError()` (#3671)
