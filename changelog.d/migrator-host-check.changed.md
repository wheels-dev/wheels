- The migrator dev-tool endpoints and the GUI pages that issue their anti-CSRF token now require the request's `Host` header to name the local machine, in addition to the existing loopback-socket and no-forwarded-client checks. `localhost`, any `*.localhost` name (RFC 6761), `127.0.0.0/8`, and `[::1]` are accepted by default. **If you reach the migrator under a custom local hostname (for example `myapp.test` via `/etc/hosts`, or a Valet/Herd-style `*.test`), it now returns `403` until you allow it** with `set(migratorAllowedHosts="myapp.test")` (comma-delimited, empty by default). The `403` body states this fix.
