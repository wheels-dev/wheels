- On SQLite, `t.bigInteger()` now declares `BIGINT` instead of `INTEGER`, so the column is bound as a 64-bit integer. Values above 2,147,483,647 used to fail on Adobe ColdFusion and were silently changed on Lucee (clamped to 2,147,483,647) and BoxLang (wrapped). Existing columns keep `INTEGER` until re-declared with `changeColumn(columnType="biginteger")`; a `biginteger` primary key stays `INTEGER PRIMARY KEY`, SQLite's rowid (#4089)
