- Validation `condition` / `unless` now handles the expression shapes that used to evaluate silently. A bare name, on its own or on the left of a comparison (`condition="isActive"`, `condition="status != 'draft'"`), is resolved as `this.<name>` (a property value or a method call) when the record has it. Before, a lone name evaluated to `false` and skipped the rule, and a left-hand name was compared as its own text, so the rule always ran. A name the record doesn't have, a `this.` reference on the right of a comparison, or an expression that isn't one value or a complete `left operator right` comparison now throws `Wheels.InvalidValidationCondition`. A lone `true` or `false` evaluates to its value, and a bare word on the right of a comparison stays a string literal. The validation guide now documents the supported forms (#3928)
