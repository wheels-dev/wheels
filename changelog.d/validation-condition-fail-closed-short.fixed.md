- A validation `condition` / `unless` that is neither a reference nor a complete `left operator right` comparison now throws `Wheels.InvalidValidationCondition` instead of evaluating silently. A single bare word (`condition="isActive"`) evaluated to `false` and skipped the rule, a bare word operand (`status != 'draft'`) was compared as the literal word, a `this.` reference on the right side was compared as text, and extra tokens were ignored. A lone `true` or `false` now evaluates to its value. **Conditions written these ways never did what they say: rewrite them with `this.` (for example `condition="this.isActive"` or `condition="this.status != 'draft'"`).** The validation guide's example is corrected and the supported expression forms are documented (#3928)
