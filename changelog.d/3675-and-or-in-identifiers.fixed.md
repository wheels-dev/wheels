- `where` clauses no longer split on an uppercase `AND` / `OR` inside an identifier: a table-qualified condition such as `ORDER_AND_ITEMS.id = 1` (or a paginated `findAll()` on such a model) resolved a bogus `_ITEMS.id` property and threw `Wheels.ColumnNotFound`, and a condition starting with a property like `ORDER_NO` lost its leading `OR`. Only standalone `AND` / `OR` keywords now separate conditions (#3675)
