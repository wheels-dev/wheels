- On Adobe ColdFusion 2025 without the optional graphqlclient package, the engine's pre-start "The graphqlclient package is not installed." notice is no longer rendered as an error page: `onError()` recognizes that exact notice, logs it to wheels.log, and returns. Existing apps can add the guard by pasting the block at the top of `onError()` from the current `public/Application.cfc` template (#3726)
