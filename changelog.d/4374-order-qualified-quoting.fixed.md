- `order="table.column"` (and `order="table.column DESC"`) is now quoted the same way as `order="column"`, so a table or column whose name is a reserved word works in the qualified form too. The table and column are matched, case-insensitively, against the model and its included associations and written under their real names; a qualifier that isn't one of those tables, such as an alias, is still passed through as written (#4374).
