- Generated apps no longer prepend blank lines to every response. The per-request and lifecycle event templates (`app/events/onrequeststart.cfm`, `onrequestend.cfm`, `onsessionstart.cfm` and siblings) are `$include`d straight into the response buffer, where a bare `cfscript` block and a trailing newline leaked whitespace before the action's output — enough to make `renderText()` of an XML document ill-formed, since the `<?xml …?>` declaration must be the first byte. The templates are now wrapped in `cfsilent` with no trailing newline. ([#3882](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/3882))
