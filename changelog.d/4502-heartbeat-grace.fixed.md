- `heartbeat()` now shortens how long a dead worker's job goes unnoticed. Before, a job was only reclaimed after twice its timeout whether it heartbeated or not, so heartbeats changed nothing. Now a job that has heartbeated is reclaimed once its heartbeats stop for its heartbeat grace, `set(jobsHeartbeatGraceSeconds = n)` (default 300; `0` turns it off; values below 30 are raised to 30) or the job's own `this.heartbeatGrace`, even inside its timeout. Jobs that never heartbeat behave exactly as before. Heartbeat at least every grace / 3. A stopped heartbeat isn't proof the worker died: a `perform()` stuck in a long call that can't heartbeat is reclaimed too. `heartbeat()` still never extends a job's timeout, which stays the hard cap on one attempt. An exclusive job's heartbeat now renews its lease by the grace window, so a job that stops heartbeating loses its lease on the same clock it's reclaimed on. ([#4502](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/4502))
