- `singularize()` no longer mangles ordinary words ending in `i`: `safari`, `spaghetti`, `pi` and `api` previously became `safarus`, `spaghettus`, `pus` and `apus` (and `virus`/`octopus` lost their trailing `s`) because the Latin-plural rule was written as the character class `([octop|vir])i$` instead of an alternation. The rule is now `(octop|vir|cact|radi|foc)(us|i)$`, so `octopi`, `viri`, `cacti`, `radii` and `foci` still singularize to their `-us` forms.
