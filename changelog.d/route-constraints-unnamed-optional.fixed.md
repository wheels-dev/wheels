- Routing: typed constraint helpers (`whereNumber()`, `whereAlpha()`, `whereMatch()`, etc.) chained after an **unnamed** route with optional segments now constrain every variant that route registers. Previously only the last-registered (shortest) variant was inspected, so `.get(pattern="archive(/[year])", to="archive##index").whereNumber("year")` constrained nothing and `/archive/abc` dispatched with `year="abc"`, and `.get(pattern="posts/[id](.[format])", ...).whereNumber("id")` left `/posts/abc.json` unconstrained. Named routes were unaffected.
