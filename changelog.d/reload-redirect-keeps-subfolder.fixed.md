- On an app served under a subfolder (`set(subpath=...)` or an IIS application), `?reload=true` now redirects back inside the app: `public/Application.cfc`'s `$buildRedirectUrl()` passes the path through the framework's `$reloadRedirectPath()`, which puts the subfolder back. **Existing apps** must apply the same edit to their own `public/Application.cfc`; the snippet is in the 4.1 → 4.2 upgrade guide (#3948).
