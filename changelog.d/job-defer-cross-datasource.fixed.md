- A job enqueued inside a Wheels-managed transaction while a tenant datasource is active is now written to the job store when that transaction commits, and dropped if it rolls back. Before, depending on the engine, such a job could outlive a rolled-back transaction (BoxLang), be written to the tenant's database where no worker reads it (RustCFML), or not be written at all (Adobe ColdFusion). `enqueue()` returns `{status: "deferred", deferred: true}` in that case. On the application's own datasource, a job still joins the open transaction
