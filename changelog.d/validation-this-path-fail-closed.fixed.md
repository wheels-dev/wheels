- Validation `condition` / `unless` expressions that use a `this.` reference now fail closed for forms the evaluator never really supported, instead of silently skipping or mis-running the rule: a `this.` reference on the **right** of a comparison (e.g. `this.total gt this.limit`), and the word-form operators **`is`**, **`and`** and **`or`** on the `this.` path, now throw `Wheels.InvalidValidationCondition` naming the supported replacement. **Upgrade note:** replace `is` with `eq` (or `==`), `and` with `&&`, `or` with `||`, and put any `this.` reference on the left side of a comparison. ([#3964](https://github.com/wheels-dev/wheels/issues/3964))
