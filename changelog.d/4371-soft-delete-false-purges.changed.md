- `delete()`, `deleteAll()`, `deleteOne()` and `deleteByKey()` with `softDelete=false` are always real deletes. They now also remove rows that were already soft-deleted, and the `dependent=` children of a deleted record, without `includeSoftDeletes=true`. An explicit `includeSoftDeletes=false` no longer keeps them. With `instantiate=true` those rows now run their delete callbacks too (#4371)
